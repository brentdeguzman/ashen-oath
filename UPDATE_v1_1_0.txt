ASHEN OATH v1.1.0 — SEVEN DUNGEON BESTIARIES
=============================================

Built from the compact v3.26.1 build, published on GitHub as base release v1.0.0.

* 70 approved creatures: 10 for each dungeon biome.
* Six real sprite frames for each monster (four articulated idle frames, attack and hit).
* Unique 70 named actions and tuned combat roles; damage, control, healing, warding and mana drain.
* Biome-specific encounters for Blackroot, Duskfen, Crystal Hollow, Ashen Spire, Trial of Echoes, Sunken Shrine and Stormpeak.
* Existing 12 world-map enemies and their visuals are unchanged.
* Elemental weaknesses and resistances for dungeon monsters; hover an enemy entry in combat to inspect.
* Stage/depth weighting: later dungeon floors encounter harder and rarer monsters more often.
* Damage-response, on-hit, defeat fade and magic particle animation.
* Dungeon supply drops: a small chance to find an extra Healing Tonic.
* Saved games retain all inventory, gear, story and boss progress.

The approved concept art was used to build the sprite frames; the original concept board is not loaded at runtime.
