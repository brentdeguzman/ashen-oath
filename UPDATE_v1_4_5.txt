ASHEN OATH v1.4.5 — BACKSTAB BALANCE HOTFIX
================================================

Backstab's updated physical damage, before shared weapon, critical, elemental and ward modifiers:
  max(1, floor((ATK + floor(SPD / 2) + random integer 0..4 + 2) * 2 + 1 - enemy DEF * 0.5))

* MP cost remains 6.
* One-turn cooldown remains unchanged.
* A successful critical deals 2x damage, as for other skills.
* Assassin's Dagger's +80% Backstab boost still applies once.
* Enemy elemental resistances and wards still apply normally.
* No other skill, gear, class, monster or map balance has changed.
