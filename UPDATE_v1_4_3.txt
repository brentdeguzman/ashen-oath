ASHEN OATH v1.4.3 — ONE-TURN SKILL COOLDOWNS & OVERWORLD HP

* Every player skill has one personal turn of cooldown after use.
  A skill used on a hero's turn N is unavailable on that hero's next turn,
  then recharges in time for turn N+2. Other skills remain available.
* Skill menu visibly marks unavailable skills as CD 1 TURN.
* Skipped turns (status conditions and Asura recovery) count toward recovery.
* All outdoor/overworld roaming enemies have 10% less HP than v1.4.2.
  Dungeon monsters and guardian bosses are unchanged.
* Combat damage, skill MP costs, gear, refinery, dungeon progression,
  and saved data remain otherwise unchanged.
* Battle cooldowns reset at the start of each encounter and aren't saved.
