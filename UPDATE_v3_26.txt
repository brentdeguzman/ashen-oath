ASHEN OATH v3.26 — MERCHANT & ITEM MANAGEMENT
================================================

* Talk to Torren in Briarwatch's Ironwood Workshop to Buy or Sell.
* His shop now has Buy Equipment and Sell From Bag tabs.
* Sell individual duplicate gear pieces safely by item instance.
* Sell gear for 50% of catalog value, rounded down, minimum 1 gold.
* Sell Healing Tonics for 6 gold each (1 or all at once).
* Drop gear permanently from the Inventory tab or drop 1/all tonics.
* Confirmations warn when items are equipped or unique.
* Automatically unequips sold/dropped equipment from any party member.
* Gold, tonic counts, equipped stats, and inventory refresh immediately.
* Game save and import format remain compatible with v3.25.

Dropping discards items permanently; nothing is left on the ground.
