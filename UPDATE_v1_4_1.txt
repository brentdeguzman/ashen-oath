ASHEN OATH v1.4.1 — EQUIPMENT TUNING & ACCESSORY ART
====================================================
• Assassin skill Throw Dagger replaced by Poison Dagger. Single-target physical damage, independent CRIT roll, and a 40% chance to inflict 2 turns of poison. Uses the existing poisonous dagger VFX.
• MES Knife's +50% skill-damage passive is now attached to Poison Dagger.
• All armor critical chance bonuses removed. Armor HP bonuses reduced by 30%, rounded to the nearest HP.
• All accessories, including Tidewarden Amulet and Echo Crown, no longer provide bonus HP. Accessory CRIT bonuses remain.
• Normal encounter enemies gain 30% more maximum/current HP relative to v1.4.0 AFTER the existing 20% monster-stat multiplier. Boss HP is unchanged from v1.4.0.
• Accessory icons were redesigned to use richly shaded fantasy pixel-art rings, charms, necklaces, earrings, and shields matching the armor. All old IDs remain compatible with saves and +0..+7 refinement.
