ASHEN OATH v1.2.6 — DISTINCT DUNGEON SETPIECES
================================================

* Replaces the seven recycled placeholder landmark sprites with 42 large, distinct biome-specific scenery assets.

* Swamp: sunken buildings, slime-infested ruins, barge wrecks.
* Crystal: excavation equipment, mine carts, ore smelters and giant mine relics.
* Spire: ruins, iron forges, molten crucibles and battle monuments.

* All previous maps, enemy pools, boss fights, campfires, chests, exits and puzzle levers preserved.

Additional: 42 original biome-specific floor debris sprites and 14 walkable ground murals.

Graphics are drawn in the actual HTML5 canvas, not screenshots or painted backgrounds.
