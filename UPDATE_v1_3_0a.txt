ASHEN OATH v1.3.0a — BLACKROOT FOREST EDGES (VERIFIED HOTFIX)

Corrected a JavaScript scope error in the first v1.3.0 package that prevented the Blackroot modifications from running. The corrected patch is within the game script scope.

Changes:
* 29 additional traversable tiles across the forest dungeon's clearings.
* Dense bushes, fern-covered rims, hanging roots and tree silhouettes along the forest cliff edges.
* Minor forest boss-room border adjustments (3 tiles).
* Existing stair crossings and progression were kept. No new elevation tiers were introduced in this hotfix.

This is a targeted expansion/visual pass, not yet a complete terrain-art or floor-layout redesign on the scale of Crystal Hollow v1.2.9.

Browser proof images and the separate captured video are provided alongside the ZIP in the conversation.
