ASHEN OATH v1.2.0 — COMBAT ARTS & GUARDIAN BALANCE

18 unique hero skill artworks. All 9 existing art signatures are retained. Nine other skills now have original image art, with revised green Cleanse and golden Prayer artwork.
The old procedural effect is never drawn simultaneously with the new hero art. Enemy magic is unaffected. Ordinary physical attacks receive a painted slash.
Ordinary enemies deal an additional 20% damage (old 1.20x => new 1.44x total). Bosses now use an independent 1.82x base-damage multiplier.
Boss HP progression by expected campaign challenge: Briarhorn 500, Crystal Sentinel 750, Fen Devourer 1000, optional Tidebound Warden 1250, Stormclaw Gryphon 1500, Ashen Sovereign 1750, optional Keeper of Echoes 2000.
Bosses must attack on their first and every third turn, and cannot heal/ward in consecutive turns.
No changes to dungeon layouts, loot, save format or overworld enemies.
Guardian targeting preferentially presses an injured hero unless a Paladin taunt draws it away.
